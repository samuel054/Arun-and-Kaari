# Arun & Kaari

A cinematic Tamil adventure series rooted in rural Trichy, Kallanai and the Cauvery landscape.

## Current Production

**Season 1:** The Hidden Mountain Chronicles  
**Episode 1:** The Journey Begins

Arun, a curious 12-year-old boy, and Kaari, his loyal Indian Pariah Dog companion, begin an adventure connecting Tamil Nadu's landscapes, history, folklore and nature.

## Visual Direction

- Authentic Tamil Nadu locations and culture
- Rural Trichy and Kallanai as the primary visual foundation
- Warm cinematic natural lighting
- Clean 16:9 storyboard frames with no text overlays
- Consistent Arun, Kaari, Grandfather and Guardian designs
- Every storyboard frame doubles as a Google Flow visual reference

## Episode 1 Workflow

1. Lock the complete Tamil screenplay
2. Build the cinematic shot list
3. Generate final clean storyboard frames
4. Animate each shot in Google Flow
5. Add Tamil voices, ambience, Foley and music
6. Edit and publish the completed episode
