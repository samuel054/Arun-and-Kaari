# Character Bible

## Arun
- Age: 12
- Origin: Rural Trichy, Tamil Nadu
- Build: Slim, active
- Skin: Warm medium-brown
- Hair: Thick black, slightly wavy and naturally messy
- Eyes: Dark brown
- Default outfit: Maroon cotton T-shirt, dark blue knee-length shorts, brown sandals, black thread necklace
- Adventure accessory: Small brown sling bag or olive backpack, depending on sequence
- Personality: Curious first, brave second; kind, observant, expressive
- Acting rule: Looks with his eyes before moving; rarely stands completely still

## Kaari
- Breed: Indian Pariah Dog / Nattu Nai
- Age: 3
- Build: Lean, agile, medium-sized
- Coat: Tan with cream chest and underside
- Ears: Upright
- Tail: Curled upward
- Eyes: Amber-brown
- Collar: Dark brown with brass name tag
- Personality: Loyal, playful, protective, alert to danger
- Acting rule: Notices changes before Arun and communicates through ears, tail, posture and barking

## Grandfather
- Age: 72
- Role: Retired farmer, storyteller, guardian of local history
- Appearance: White hair, trimmed white beard and moustache, round spectacles
- Clothing: Cream/white cotton shirt, white veshti, checked shoulder cloth, simple sandals
- Personality: Wise, patient, humorous, emotionally restrained

## The Guardian
- Height: Approximately 9.5 feet
- Form: Ancient stone protector inspired by Chola temple sculpture
- Materials: Granite, moss, vines, faint golden-blue inner glow
- Personality: Calm, powerful, protective—not a monster
- Symbolism: Nature, memory, responsibility and Tamil heritage

## Consistency Rules
- Do not change faces, body proportions, clothing colors, fur markings or accessories without a story reason.
- Use the same master references for every image and Google Flow shot.
- Lighting may change; identity colors and proportions must not.
