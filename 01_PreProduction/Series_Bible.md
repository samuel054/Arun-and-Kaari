# Arun & Kaari — Series Bible

## Core Concept
A Tamil adventure series following Arun, a curious 12-year-old boy from rural Trichy, and Kaari, his loyal Indian Pariah Dog companion. Together they explore landscapes, history, folklore, and mysteries rooted in Tamil Nadu.

## Series Identity
- Genre: Adventure, mystery, family, cultural fantasy
- Language: Tamil
- Primary setting: Trichy, Kallanai, Cauvery river basin, rural Tamil Nadu
- Audience: Children and families
- Tone: Warm, cinematic, emotional, suspenseful, culturally grounded

## Main Characters
- Arun: Curious, brave, kind, observant, slightly impatient
- Kaari: Loyal, intelligent, playful, alert, protective
- Grandfather: Wise storyteller and keeper of local history
- The Guardian: Ancient Chola-inspired protector of nature and heritage
