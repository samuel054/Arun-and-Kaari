# Environment Bible

## Geographic Foundation
The world is rooted in real rural landscapes around Trichy and Kallanai, Tamil Nadu. Environments should feel recognizably South Indian rather than generic tropical fantasy.

## Core Locations

### Arun's Village
Traditional tiled-roof houses, kolam at entrances, neem and banyan trees, bicycles, cows, goats, clay pots, temple sounds, kitchen smoke and red-earth lanes.

### Paddy Fields Near Kallanai
Bright rice fields, narrow earthen bunds, irrigation channels, banana groves, coconut rows, white egrets and distant rocky hills.

### Kallanai / Grand Anicut
Ancient weathered granite barrage across the Cauvery, rushing water, sandbanks, fishermen, coracles, river birds and Chola-era visual influence.

### Cauvery Riverside
Banyan roots, reeds, smooth rocks, shallow flowing water, kingfishers, dragonflies and morning mist.

### Rural Forest Trail
Dry deciduous woodland with tamarind, neem, banyan, Indian fig, thorn shrubs, bamboo patches and filtered sunlight. Avoid rainforest imagery.

### Hidden Chola Temple
Granite pillars, weathered Tamil inscriptions, moss, creepers, carved motifs and tree roots embracing ruins.

### Crystal Cave
Fictional granite cave with quartz-like crystals, reflective underground stream, cool blue-green light and restrained magical glow.

### Hidden Mountain
Rocky hill inspired by central Tamil Nadu terrain, granite boulders, wind-shaped shrubs and panoramic Cauvery valley views.

## Continuity Rules
- Keep architecture, vegetation and terrain authentic to Tamil Nadu.
- Do not insert foreign cottages, European castles or generic jungle temples.
- Reuse fixed layouts for Arun's house, village road, banyan tree and temple locations.
- Color progression: warm village amber → green mystery → cool cave blue → golden summit.
