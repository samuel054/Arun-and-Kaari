# Episode 1 — The Journey Begins

## Runtime
Target: 12–15 minutes

## Episode Goal
Introduce Arun, Kaari, Grandfather, rural Trichy, Kallanai and the mystery of the Hidden Mountain.

## Ending
Arun and Kaari reach a hilltop and see the Hidden Mountain for the first time through the mist.

## Emotional Progression
Peace → Wonder → Curiosity → Adventure → Mystery → Cliffhanger

## Sequence Plan
1. Mystery Hook
2. Morning in the Village
3. Meet Arun and Kaari
4. Grandfather's Story
5. Decision to Leave
6. Journey Begins
7. First View of the Hidden Mountain
8. Cliffhanger Ending

## Production Rule
Every final storyboard image must be a clean 16:9 cinematic frame with no text, labels or infographic layout. The same image will be used directly as a Google Flow reference.
